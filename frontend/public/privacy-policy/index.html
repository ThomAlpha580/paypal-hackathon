EasyAlpha Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EasyAlpha ("EasyAlpha," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the EasyAlpha website, applications, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account and authentication information

When you sign in using a supported identity provider, such as Google or Naver, we may receive information permitted by that provider, such as your account identifier, name, email address, and profile image. We also process authentication tokens and account security information needed to keep you signed in and protect your account. The information shared with us is controlled by your provider settings and its privacy policy.

1.2 Content you submit and results we generate

To provide the Service, we process information such as:

  • natural-language stock screening criteria and selected markets;
  • stock symbols, names, and other parameters submitted for analysis;
  • AI analysis requests, generated results, and decision records; and
  • feedback or support messages you choose to send us.

Please do not include sensitive personal information or confidential information in your screening criteria, analysis requests, or support messages unless it is necessary for us to assist you.

1.3 Usage, device, and diagnostic information

We may automatically collect IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring pages, pages or features used, timestamps, approximate location derived from IP address, performance data, and error or security logs. We use analytics services, including PostHog, to understand product usage and improve reliability. Analytics events may be associated with an account identifier after you sign in.

1.4 Local storage and cookies

EasyAlpha uses browser local storage and similar technologies to retain authentication state, basic user information, language and theme preferences, and your most recent stock-screening query. We may also use first-party cookies for interface preferences, security, analytics, and session operation. You can clear these through your browser settings, but doing so may sign you out or reset preferences.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • operate, maintain, and secure the Service;
  • authenticate users and manage accounts;
  • interpret natural-language screening requests and return candidate stocks;
  • produce AI-assisted stock analysis using market, technical, fundamental, news, filing, and other relevant information;
  • save and display screening and analysis records where applicable;
  • measure usage, troubleshoot errors, prevent fraud, and improve the Service;
  • respond to support, privacy, or legal requests; and
  • comply with applicable law and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • with hosting, database, security, analytics, authentication, market data, news, and AI service providers that process it for us;
  • with identity providers when you choose to sign in through their services;
  • when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate abuse, or prevent fraud;
  • as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate safeguards; or
  • with your direction or consent.

Stock screening criteria and analysis requests may be sent to third-party AI and data providers solely as needed to generate and support the requested output. Their processing is governed by our arrangements with them and, where applicable, their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain security and business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods vary by data type and context. We may retain de-identified or aggregated information that cannot reasonably identify you.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. However, no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. International Processing

EasyAlpha and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Data protection laws in those locations may differ from local laws. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of your personal information, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request, and legal exceptions may apply.

You can clear locally stored preferences and queries through your browser and can disconnect EasyAlpha from your identity provider through that provider's account settings. To request deletion of server-side account or analysis data, contact us using the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 18, or the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Third-Party Links and Data Sources

The Service may display or link to third-party websites, market data, filings, news, or other content. We do not control those third parties, and this Privacy Policy does not cover their independent practices.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Service or applicable law changes. We will post the revised policy here and update the date above. If required by law, we will provide additional notice.

11. Contact Us

For questions or requests concerning this Privacy Policy or your personal information, email team@findtruman.io.
